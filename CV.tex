  \item Calibrated LSV (SSVI, Dupire local vol, particle method); model risk: exotics up to 2x apart for the same vanillas

  \item Added Bermudan/American exercise (Longstaff--Schwartz) and Greeks up to vanna and volga
\end{itemize}

  \item Computed fair coupons, call/loss probabilities and \textbf{Greeks} (vega, dividends) for 35 product variants
\end{itemize}

\project{Interest Rate Curves \& Derivatives}{ESTR, EURIBOR, SOFR, Hull--White}

  \item Priced a callable SOFR range accrual (Hull--White): replication, Monte Carlo, Longstaff--Schwartz within 1 bp
